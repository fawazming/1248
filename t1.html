Tutorial / Assignment Questions

Original CSC 713 assignment questions. Model answers are in the Notes pages (n1-n6).

1) Fuzzy Logic

A digital computer is good at processing discrete data, but life challenges and situations do not always conform to discrete descriptions and management.

a) How is this situation managed in AI?

b) List and concisely describe the four principal stages of a fuzzy logic system.

c) Highlight and discuss any 2 ways Fuzzy Logic has contributed to the development of AI.

d) Given fresh data to learn from, how do fuzzy logic systems improve their problem-solving capability?

e) Involvement of Knowledge Domain Experts (KDE) can bring either joys or worries to the knowledge engineer regarding development and continuous relevance of a Fuzzy Logic system. Advance and discuss 3 points to support or oppose this statement and identify 2 solution approaches to neutralise the curse.

f) What is a major challenge common to both Expert System and Fuzzy Logic systems' developers and what solution strategies will you proffer?

2) ChatBOT, ANN and Genetic Programming

A class of 400 postgraduate students of AI grouped into 40 work teams were each required to develop a ChatBOT trained to correctly vocalise English word homonyms including: ONE, WARN, WON, WORN, NOT, NUT, NAUGHT and NORTH. The goal is to select just one BOT with the best performance (above 90% success) by using ANN for the training and by asking another AI system to critique and evaluate each candidate BOT's performance.

a) What ANN type will be most appropriate for the training and why?

b) What ANN type (or AI system) should be invoked for the evaluation?

c) What is the tree or population size for this AI challenge?

d) For this antithesis, advance a step-by-step genetic programming evolutionary process for the best performing BOT to emerge.

e) Briefly describe what obtains at each step advanced in 2(d) above.

f) Using an applicable well-labelled diagram and clear explanations, discuss Turing's proposal on AI (1950).

3) Intelligence

Intelligence is pivotal in Artificial Intelligence (AI).

a) Differentiate among AI, AI Research and Artificial Intelligence System in Computer Science.

b) List any five (5) types of human intelligence that AI might replicate.

c) State five of the activities that demonstrate human intelligence and expatiate where necessary.

d) How does strong AI differ from weak AI?

4) Search

Search is a fundamental concept in Artificial Intelligence.

i. Precisely describe Search Problem in AI.

ii. List and describe the five components of a search problem.

iii. State the requisite steps for a search process.
